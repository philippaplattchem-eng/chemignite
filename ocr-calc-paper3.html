Practice Questions

Q1. (Synoptic: Moles + Kc + Equilibrium)
1.00 mol of H$_2$ and 1.00 mol of CO$_2$ are mixed and allowed to reach equilibrium in a 5.00 dm$^3$ flask at 700 K. At equilibrium, 0.608 mol of CO is present.
$\text{H}_2\text{(g) + CO}_2\text{(g)} \rightleftharpoons \text{H}_2\text{O(g) + CO(g)}$
(a) Calculate $K_c$ at 700 K.   (b) Deduce the units of $K_c$.   (c) Calculate $K_p$ if total pressure = 200 kPa. [6]
Q2. (Synoptic: Calorimetry + Hess's Law)
The enthalpy of combustion of graphite is $-393$ kJ mol$^{-1}$ and of hydrogen is $-286$ kJ mol$^{-1}$. The enthalpy of combustion of ethanol is $-1368$ kJ mol$^{-1}$. In a separate experiment, 1.15 g of ethanol is burned in a spirit lamp and heats 250 cm$^3$ of water from 18.0 °C to 27.5 °C.
(a) Calculate the experimental $\Delta H_c$ for ethanol.
(b) Calculate the theoretical $\Delta H_f$ for ethanol using Hess's Law.
(c) Suggest two reasons why the experimental value differs from the data book value. [8]
Q3. (Synoptic: pH + Buffers + $K_w$)
A buffer solution is prepared by adding 20.0 cm$^3$ of 0.100 mol dm$^{-3}$ NaOH to 50.0 cm$^3$ of 0.100 mol dm$^{-3}$ ethanoic acid ($K_a=1.74\times10^{-5}$ mol dm$^{-3}$).
(a) Show that the mixture forms a buffer.
(b) Calculate the pH of the buffer.
(c) Calculate the pH if the temperature is raised to 50 °C where $K_w=5.47\times10^{-14}$ mol$^2$ dm$^{-6}$. Does the pH of a neutral solution increase or decrease with increasing temperature? [7]
Q4. (Synoptic: Arrhenius + Rates + Born–Haber)
A reaction has $E_a = 75.0$ kJ mol$^{-1}$. The rate constant at 300 K is $k_1 = 1.50\times10^{-4}$ s$^{-1}$.
(a) Calculate $k$ at 350 K.
(b) Explain, with reference to the Boltzmann distribution, why increasing temperature increases the rate constant. [5]

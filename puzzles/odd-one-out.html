Chemistry Puzzles — General Knowledge

Odd One Out — ANSWERS

Teacher copy — black chip = odd one out. Accept other well-reasoned answers.

1. PHOTON
Proton, neutron and electron are subatomic particles found in atoms. A photon is a particle of electromagnetic radiation (light), not part of an atom's structure.
2. CALCIUM
Lithium, sodium and potassium are Group 1 alkali metals. Calcium is a Group 2 metal (alkaline earth metal).
3. SULFUR
Fluorine, chlorine and bromine are Group 7 halogens. Sulfur is in Group 6 (Group 16) and is a non-metal but not a halogen.
4. ETHENE
Methane, ethane and propane are alkanes (general formula CnH2n+2). Ethene is an alkene (C=C double bond, general formula CnH2n).
5. NaOH
HCl, H₂SO₄ and HNO₃ are acids that donate H⁺ ions in solution. NaOH is an alkali/base — it releases OH⁻ ions and has pH > 7.
6. VAN DER WAALS
Covalent, ionic and metallic are types of primary (intramolecular) chemical bonds. Van der Waals forces are weak intermolecular forces, not a type of chemical bond.
7. SPATULA
Burette, pipette and measuring cylinder are all used to measure volumes of liquid accurately. A spatula is used to transfer solid substances — it does not measure volume.
8. FILTRATION
Oxidation, reduction and neutralisation are chemical reactions (bonds are broken and formed). Filtration is a physical separation technique — no chemical change occurs.
★ 9. NITROGEN
Helium, neon and argon are noble gases (Group 0/18) with full outer shells. Nitrogen is in Group 5 (Group 15) and forms diatomic molecules (N₂); it is not a noble gas.
★ 10. O₂
CO₂, H₂O and CO can all be products of combustion of a hydrocarbon fuel. O₂ is a reactant in combustion (it is the oxidant), not a product.

Connections Challenge 3 — ANSWERS

CHALLENGE

The meta-connection: every word in the puzzle is an adjective or noun that precedes a common chemistry term. Students must find which 4 words share the same hidden word. Key traps — NUCLEAR and THERMAL could fit in energy OR reaction; OXIDATION could fit number OR reaction; HYDROGEN could look like a reaction type.

★★☆☆   GROUP 1 — All precede "NUMBER"
Atomic number (proton count), mass number (protons + neutrons), Avogadro's number (6.02 × 10²³), oxidation number (charge in a compound).

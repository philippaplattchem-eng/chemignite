Connections Challenge 2 — ANSWERS

CHALLENGE

Key traps: IRON and VANADIUM are transition metals — students may group all four transition metals together, but IRON and VANADIUM go with catalysts, while PLATINUM belongs there too rather than with period 3. SILICON looks like a metal but is a period 3 metalloid. GRAPHITE conducts electricity — students may confuse it with a metal.

★★☆☆   GROUP 1 — Allotropes of carbon
All are different structural forms of the element carbon only. Diamond (tetrahedral lattice), graphite (layered sheets), fullerene (C₆₀ ball), graphene (single atomic sheet).

★★★★   GROUP 2 — Elements named after scientists
Einsteinium (Es) — Albert Einstein; Curium (Cm) — Marie & Pierre Curie; Fermium (Fm) — Enrico Fermi; Mendelevium (Md) — Dmitri Mendeleev.

Trap: these are real elements on the periodic table — students may just think they are obscure elements with no special connection.
★★★☆   GROUP 3 — Catalysts in major industrial processes
Iron — Haber process (ammonia synthesis); Vanadium — Contact process (sulfuric acid); Platinum — catalytic converters / Ostwald process (nitric acid); Nickel — hydrogenation of vegetable oils.

Trap: all four are transition metals, so students may group them that way — the specific connection is their role as industrial catalysts.
★★☆☆   GROUP 4 — Period 3 elements
Sodium (Na, Z=11), Magnesium (Mg, Z=12), Aluminium (Al, Z=13), Silicon (Si, Z=14) — all in the third period of the periodic table.
